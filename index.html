An interactive book, rebuilt from first principles

This is an original recreation of the interactive book renderer seen on Stripe Press — not a copy of their artwork or copy, but the same underlying technique: a real 3D book mesh, a procedurally generated foil-stamped cover, image-based lighting for gold reflections, and a subtle pointer-driven tilt that follows your cursor.

Every marbled pattern is generated at load time by a small flow field noise algorithm, then split into a diffuse layer, a bump layer, and a metallic “foil” mask — the same layering a real foil-stamped hardcover uses, just simulated in a shader instead of etched in copper.

Built with three.js · procedural textures · no external assets
